06

Motion, picture, sound

Motion

  • cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1) on everything — the site's own curve. Fast in, long settle, no bounce.
  • Entrances ~0.45s. Cards wipe left to right so a speaker change is visible.
  • Nothing is still animating when the card is meant to be read.

Picture

  • Cut on detected silences, never on SRT cue boundaries — cues run through sentence ends and clip words.
  • People appear in their full source frame. Cropping in cuts heads and shoulders.
  • Centre every card on one axis so consecutive beats don't jump.
  • Meeting spotlight view lags the speaker switch (1s+) — grab the frame before trusting a timestamp.

Sound

  • Find boundaries with silencedetect=n=-34dB:d=0.16, then confirm each one by cutting it and re-transcribing for content.
  • Leave about 120ms of air before the first word and 180ms after the last consonant. Less clips the word; more reads as a stall.
  • Never cut on a consonant. "a lot of opport—", "every two wee—" all shipped and all had to be re-cut.
  • Trim the trailing breath. The tail after a line is audible even when the meter looks flat.
  • A fade does not hide the next phrase. If a following sentence leaks in, shorten the clip — don't ramp the volume.
  • Start a quote on the phrase that introduces it, not on the quote itself, or the line lands as a fragment.
  • Every beat needs a verbal hand-off. A freeze with no audio bridge reads as lag, not as a pause.
  • Whisper word timings shift with the clip window. Cut to final length first, read cues off that exact file, bias ~60ms early.
  • Master at --crf=14, around −14 LUFS, with a CRF-23 +faststart web copy alongside.
